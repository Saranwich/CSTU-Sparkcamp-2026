# Five Nights at Freddy's 4 (FNAF 4)

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
The first game to take place entirely outside of a commercial location. Players control a young child in their bedroom, defending themselves against terrifying **Nightmare** versions of the classic characters. Survival depends on listening for breathing at the doors, checking the bed, and monitoring the closet.

The gameplay is deeply psychological and relies heavily on audio cues, as the child is presumably experiencing these horrors in a state of fear and trauma.

## Key Lore Points
- **The Bite of '83:** The game's minigames depict the tragic events leading up to a birthday party at **Fredbear's Family Diner**. The "Crying Child" (William Afton’s youngest son) is bullied by his brother, Michael, and friends. They accidentally put the child's head into Fredbear's mouth; the animatronic's jaw snaps shut, causing a fatal injury.
- **The Afton Family:** This game established that the central narrative revolves around the Afton family. It suggests that the nightmares are either the result of the child's trauma or experiments conducted by William Afton using illusion discs or gas (as hinted at in later games).
- **The Box:** A mysterious locked box appears at the end of the game with the text: "Perhaps some things are best left forgotten, for now." Its contents remain one of the series' greatest mysteries.
