# Ultimate Custom Night (UCN)

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
The ultimate crossover event in the series, featuring a roster of **50 animatronics** from all previous games. Players can customize the AI levels of each character to create the most difficult challenge in FNAF history (the infamous 50/20 mode).

While primarily a gameplay-focused title, the voice lines and secret cutscenes provide a chilling look into the afterlife of the series' antagonist.

## Game Features

| Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Roster** | 50 standard characters plus secret "Dee Dee" summons. |
| **Office Skins** | Unlockable offices based on previous games. |
| **Power-ups** | Frigid, Heater, Power Generator, Global Music Box, etc. |

## Key Lore Points
- **Afton's Purgatory:** The community consensus is that the player character is **William Afton**, trapped in an endless cycle of torment after the fire in *Pizzeria Simulator*. 
- **The One You Should Not Have Killed:** The animatronics frequently refer to "The One You Should Not Have Killed," an vengeful spirit who refuses to let Afton die. This spirit is believed to be **Cassidy** (the soul inside Golden Freddy), whose face occasionally appears in the game's static or vents.
- **Old Man Consequences Redux:** In a secret ending, the player can find Old Man Consequences again. He tells the vengeful spirit to leave Afton to his own demons and finally rest, suggesting that the spirit's quest for revenge is preventing its own peace.
