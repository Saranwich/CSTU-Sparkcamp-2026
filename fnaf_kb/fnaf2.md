# Five Nights at Freddy's 2 (FNAF 2)

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
A prequel to the original game, set in **1987**. The "Grand Re-opening" features a new, larger location and a cast of "Toy" animatronics. These new models are equipped with advanced facial recognition software linked to criminal databases to ensure customer safety. 

Players control **Jeremy Fitzgerald** (and later Fritz Smith) in an office with no doors. Survival depends on using a Freddy Fazbear Mask to fool most animatronics, a flashlight to ward off Foxy, and a music box to keep the Puppet at bay.

## Key Lore Points
- **The Bite of '87:** A catastrophic event where an animatronic bit off the frontal lobe of a human. This led to the animatronics being restricted from walking during the day and eventually the closure of the 1987 location.
- **The Puppet (Charlie Emily):** It is revealed that the Puppet is possessed by Charlotte Emily, the daughter of William Afton's business partner, Henry. She is responsible for "giving life" to the other spirits by tethering them to the animatronic suits.
- **Save Them Minigames:** These 8-bit minigames depict another series of murders at the new location, further establishing William Afton's history of violence.
