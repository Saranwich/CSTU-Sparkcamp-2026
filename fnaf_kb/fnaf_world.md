# FNAF World

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
A colorful RPG spin-off that stands in stark contrast to the horror roots of the series. Players form parties using "Adventure" versions of characters from the first four games to battle enemies in the world of **Animatronica**.

While initially criticized for its tone, the game contains deep meta-narrative layers and hidden endings that provide crucial context for the main series.

## Game Features

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Playable Characters** | 40+ characters from FNAF 1-4, plus Halloween Edition and hidden characters. |
| **Combat** | Turn-based RPG combat using chips and bytes for buffs/attacks. |
| **Meta-Elements** | Interaction with "Desk Man" and the game's creator, Scott Cawthon (represented as Animdude). |

## Key Lore Points
- **The Clock Ending:** By following the instructions of a "glitched" Fredbear, players can find hidden clocks. This ending implies that the "player" is setting up the pieces for the Crying Child (from FNAF 4) to find his way to peace, directly linking to the "Happiest Day" minigames in FNAF 3.
- **Old Man Consequences:** The introduction of a mysterious red figure in a lake. He tells the player (or a spirit) that they have gone too deep into the "code" and should rest. This character reappears in *Ultimate Custom Night*.
- **The Fourth Wall:** The game frequently breaks the fourth wall, addressing the nature of the franchise, the expectations of the fans, and the toll the series has taken on its creator.
