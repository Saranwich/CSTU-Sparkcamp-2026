# Five Nights at Freddy's (FNAF 1)

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
The game that started it all. Set in the 1990s, players assume the role of **Mike Schmidt**, a night security guard at **Freddy Fazbear's Pizza**. The goal is to survive five nights (plus extra shifts) while managing limited electrical power to operate security cameras, lights, and steel doors.

The animatronics are programmed with a "free-roaming mode" at night. Due to a design flaw (or supernatural influence), they perceive the guard as a metal endoskeleton without a suit and will attempt to forcefully stuff the player into a Freddy Fazbear mascot suit, resulting in death.

## Character Roster

| Animatronic | Role | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Freddy Fazbear** | Lead Singer | Active in later nights; hides in shadows and attacks from the right door. |
| **Bonnie the Bunny** | Guitarist | Aggressive; always attacks from the left door. |
| **Chica the Chicken** | Backup Singer | Persistent; attacks from the right door; often found in the kitchen. |
| **Foxy the Pirate Fox** | Out-of-Order | Located in Pirate Cove; sprints down the left hallway if not monitored. |
| **Golden Freddy** | Secret | A spectral entity that appears in the office; triggers a game crash if looked at. |

## Key Lore Points
- **The Missing Children Incident (MCI):** Five children were lured into the back room by an individual wearing a Spring Bonnie mascot suit. Their bodies were never found, but the restaurant was plagued by reports of blood and mucus oozing from the animatronics.
- **The Possession:** It is heavily implied that the spirits of the murdered children inhabit the four main animatronics, seeking vengeance against their killer.
- **The "Phone Guy":** A previous employee who provides guidance via recorded messages, eventually meeting his demise on Night 4.
