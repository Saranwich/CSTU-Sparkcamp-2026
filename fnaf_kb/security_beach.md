# FNAF: Security Breach

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
The most ambitious title in the series, featuring a free-roam environment within the massive **Freddy Fazbear’s Mega Pizzaplex**. Players control **Gregory**, a young boy who is trapped in the mall after hours.

In a twist for the series, **Glamrock Freddy** becomes Gregory's ally and protector, helping him navigate the mall and evade the other "hacked" animatronics and the security guard, Vanessa.

## Key Lore Points
- **The Pizzaplex Era:** Fazbear Entertainment has fully transitioned into a corporate titan. They have built the Pizzaplex directly on top of the remains of the FNAF 6 location, attempting to literally bury their past.
- **The Mimic:** While initially appearing to be a revived William Afton (Burntrap), expanded lore from the *Tales from the Pizzaplex* book series reveals the existence of **The Mimic**—an AI designed to copy behavior that has been infected by a malicious "Agony" virus.
- **The Multiple Endings:** The game features several endings, ranging from Gregory and Freddy escaping together to the total destruction of the Pizzaplex and the return of a blob-like entity known as **The Tangle**, which contains parts of many previous animatronics.
