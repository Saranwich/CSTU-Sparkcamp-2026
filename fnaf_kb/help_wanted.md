# FNAF: Help Wanted (VR)

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
A Virtual Reality experience (later released for flat screens) that meta-fictionalizes the entire franchise. In the game's universe, **Fazbear Entertainment** has hired an indie developer to create a VR game that mocks the "urban legends" of their past in an attempt to rehabilitate their brand.

However, during development, the studio scanned old circuit boards to speed up the process, accidentally digitizing a dormant and dangerous entity.

## Game Modules

| Module | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **FNAF 1-3** | Reimagined versions of the original games in VR. |
| **Parts & Service** | Maintenance mini-games with Freddy, Bonnie, Chica, and Foxy. |
| **Vent Repair** | High-tension repairs while fending off Mangle and Ennard. |
| **Night Terrors** | Defending a bedroom from Nightmare animatronics. |

## Key Lore Points
- **Glitchtrap:** A digital manifestation of **William Afton** (or a complex AI mimicry of him). He appears as a raggedy, stitched-together bunny suit that watches the player from the shadows, gradually becoming more present as the player collects tapes.
- **The Tape Girl:** Hidden recordings left by a beta tester who discovered Glitchtrap. She explains how the virus is attempting to "attach" itself to the player to escape the digital world.
- **Vanny (Vanessa):** The game ends with the implication that Glitchtrap has successfully influenced or merged with a beta tester named Vanessa. She takes on the mantle of **Vanny**, a reluctant follower of Afton, setting the stage for the events of *Security Breach*.
