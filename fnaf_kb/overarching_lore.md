# FNAF: Overarching Lore & Mysteries

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

This document explores the foundational elements that tie the **Five Nights at Freddy's** universe together, from the tragic Afton family to the supernatural science of Remnant.

## 1. The Afton Family

The central tragedy of the series is driven by the Afton family, led by the antagonist William Afton.

| Member | Known As | Fate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **William Afton** | Purple Guy / Springtrap | Trapped in a springlock suit; later digitizes his consciousness. |
| **Michael Afton** | The Protagonist | Becomes a living corpse; dies in the FNAF 6 fire to end his father's legacy. |
| **Elizabeth Afton** | Circus Baby | Killed by Baby's claw; eventually ejected from Ennard. |
| **The Crying Child** | Bite Victim | Died in the hospital after the "Bite of '83"; his soul remains a mystery. |

## 2. Remnant and Agony

The supernatural occurrences in the series are explained through two primary pseudo-scientific concepts:

- **Remnant:** A substance created by the fusion of a human soul with metal. It is typically harvested through extreme heat and is what allows spirits to possess machinery and potentially achieve immortality.
- **Agony:** A powerful form of emotional energy. High-intensity negative emotions (like the trauma of the MCI) can infect objects, causing them to behave as if they were alive, even without a literal soul inhabiting them.

## 3. The Core Timeline (Simplified)

1.  **The Origins (1970s - 1983):** Henry Emily and William Afton open Fredbear's Family Diner. The "Bite of '83" occurs.
2.  **The Killing Spree (1985 - 1987):** William begins murdering children at various Freddy Fazbear's locations. The "Bite of '87" leads to the Toy models' retirement.
3.  **The Long Wait (1990s - 2023):** Michael Afton searches for his father. William is "killed" by his victims and becomes Springtrap.
4.  **The Resolution (Post-2023):** Henry Emily lures all remaining entities to a fake pizzeria and burns it to the ground.
5.  **The Digital Era (Modern Day):** Fazbear Entertainment attempts a comeback, leading to the rise of Glitchtrap and the events at the Mega Pizzaplex.

## 4. Key Mysteries

- **Midnight Motorist:** Who is the "Orange Man," and where is the child running off to?
- **Golden Freddy's Identity:** Is it possessed by one soul (Cassidy) or two (Cassidy and the Crying Child)?
- **The Mimic vs. Afton:** Is the entity in the Pizzaplex truly William Afton, or is it a machine that has simply learned to mimic his evil?
