# Five Nights at Freddy's 3 (FNAF 3)

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
Set 30 years after the events of FNAF 1, the story moves to **Fazbear's Fright: The Horror Attraction**. The creators of the attraction have scavenged old pizzeria locations for authentic relics. They discover a single, functioning animatronic: a heavily decayed suit known as **Springtrap**.

The player must monitor the building's systems (Audio, Camera, Ventilation) while luring Springtrap away from the office using audio cues. Failure to maintain systems leads to hallucinations and Springtrap's eventual arrival.

## Key Lore Points
- **William Afton's Fate:** In a series of post-night minigames, it is shown that William Afton returned to the FNAF 1 location to dismantle the animatronics. The spirits of his victims cornered him in the safe room. To escape, he hid inside the old **Spring Bonnie** suit. The moisture in the room caused the springlocks to fail, crushing him inside. However, due to his "Remnant," he remained "alive" within the suit for decades.
- **The Happiest Day:** By completing secret minigames, the player can trigger the "Happiest Day" ending, where the spirits of the children finally find peace and move on.
- **The Fire:** The attraction burns down at the end of the week. While most items are lost, a newspaper clipping reveals that Springtrap survived the blaze.
