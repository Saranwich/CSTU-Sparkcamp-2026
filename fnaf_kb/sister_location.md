# FNAF: Sister Location

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
A major shift in gameplay and storytelling, set in the underground **Circus Baby's Entertainment and Rental** facility. Unlike previous games, the player (Michael Afton) moves between different rooms each night to perform specific maintenance tasks under the guidance of **HandUnit** and **Circus Baby**.

The facility was designed by **Afton Robotics** to house animatronics that, while appearing as entertainers, were secretly built with features intended for capturing children.

## Character Roster

| Animatronic | Features | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Circus Baby** | Claw mechanism, ice cream dispenser | Possessed by Elizabeth Afton. |
| **Ballora** | Sound-based tracking | A ballerina-style animatronic. |
| **Funtime Freddy** | Proximity sensor, Bon-Bon puppet | Highly aggressive and erratic. |
| **Funtime Foxy** | Motion-activated | Located in the Funtime Auditorium. |
| **Ennard** | An amalgam of all endoskeletons | The "final boss" of the main game. |

## Key Lore Points
- **Elizabeth Afton:** William's daughter was killed by Circus Baby's internal claw mechanism after being left alone with the animatronic, despite her father's warnings. Her spirit subsequently possessed Baby.
- **Remnant:** This game introduces the concept of Remnant—a substance derived from human souls and metal that allows for a form of immortality or "haunting" of machines. William Afton was experimenting with this substance to achieve eternal life.
- **Michael’s Transformation:** On the final night, Michael is led to the "Scooper" room, where his internal organs are removed so that **Ennard** can use his body as a "skin-suit" to escape the facility. Michael eventually ejects Ennard and survives as a "purple" living corpse, fueled by Remnant, and begins his quest to find his father.
