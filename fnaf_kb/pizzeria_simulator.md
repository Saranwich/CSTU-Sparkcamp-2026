# FNAF: Pizzeria Simulator (FNAF 6)

**Navigation:** [Index](_index.md) | [Overarching Lore](overarching_lore.md)

## Summary
Disguised as a lighthearted business tycoon game, **Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria Simulator** serves as the definitive conclusion to the original story arc. Players manage a new pizzeria during the day—buying furniture and games—while performing maintenance tasks in the vents at night.

The player is also tasked with "salvaging" old animatronics found in the alleyway. These animatronics are highly dangerous and are the key to the game's true purpose.

## Key Lore Points
- **Henry Emily:** William’s former partner returns to the narrative. He is the mastermind behind the "fake" pizzeria, designed as a labyrinth to trap all the remaining possessed animatronics in one location.
- **The Puppet's Fate:** Henry reveals that Lefty (L.E.F.T.E. - Lure Encapsulate Fuse Transport & Extract) was built specifically to find and contain his daughter, Charlie (The Puppet), who had been wandering for decades.
- **The Final Fire:** In the "Completion" ending, Henry sets the facility ablaze with all the animatronics, Michael Afton, and himself inside. He delivers a famous monologue, declaring that the souls can finally rest, while William Afton is condemned to "the darkest pit of hell."
